Video Publisher

An open-source desktop application for Windows that publishes one video to several social media platforms at once: YouTube (including Shorts), Facebook Pages and Telegram channels.

Publish now or schedule for later, and keep the history of your publications in one place.

How Video Publisher uses your accounts

  • YouTube: after you log in with Google, the application uploads the videos you choose to your own channel, can schedule them, and can delete a video it scheduled if you cancel the publication. It reads your channel name only to show which channel you are connected to.
  • Facebook: publishes videos to a Page you manage.
  • Telegram: posts videos to your channel through your bot or your account.

The application runs on your computer. Your videos, texts and login keys stay on your computer and are sent only to the platforms you publish to. The developer does not receive or store your data. You can disconnect any account in the application's settings at any time.
