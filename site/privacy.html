Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Video Publisher ("the Application", "we") handles information when you use the Video Publisher desktop application for Windows. The Application publishes videos you choose to your own accounts on YouTube, Facebook and Telegram.

In short: the Application runs on your computer. Your videos, texts, settings and login keys stay on your computer and are sent only to the platforms you choose to publish to. We (the developer) do not operate servers that receive your data, and we do not collect, sell or share it.

1. YouTube API Services

The Application uses YouTube API Services to upload videos to your YouTube channel. By using the YouTube features of the Application, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of your information is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

2. Information the Application Accesses

  • Video files you select for publishing, and their duration and frame size (read on your computer to tell whether a video is a YouTube Short).
  • Texts and settings you enter: titles, descriptions, hashtags, tags, category, visibility, the "made for kids" setting and publication times.
  • From your Google account (permission https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube): the ID and title of your YouTube channel, and a refresh token that lets the Application upload videos and delete videos it scheduled, without asking you to log in each time. The Application does not read your email, contacts, other videos, comments or analytics.
  • From Facebook: the ID and name of the Page you choose and an access token for that Page.
  • From Telegram: your bot token or your account session, and the channel you publish to.
  • Publication history: which video went to which platform, when, its status and the link to the published post.

3. How the Information Is Used

Information is used only to publish and schedule the videos you choose, to show which account you are connected to, and to show your publication history. The Application performs actions on a platform only when you start them: publishing, scheduling, or deleting a scheduled video by removing it from the history.

The Application's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

4. Where Information Is Stored

  • Login keys (refresh token, access tokens, Telegram session, client secrets) are stored in Windows Credential Manager on your computer.
  • Settings and publication history are stored in a local database in the .video-publisher folder in your user profile.
  • Video files are not copied: the Application reads them from where you keep them.

The Application does not use cookies, analytics or advertising, and does not send information to the developer. Protecting access to your computer is your responsibility.

5. Sharing

Videos and their texts are sent only to the platforms you select, at the moment you publish or schedule them: YouTube (Google), Facebook (Meta) or Telegram. Each platform processes them under its own policy:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Meta Privacy Policy
  • Telegram Privacy Policy

We do not share information with anyone else.

6. Revoking Access and Deleting Data

  • In the Application, Settings → YouTube → Log out deletes the stored Google refresh token and channel details from your computer. The same exists for Facebook and Telegram.
  • You can revoke the Application's access to your Google account at any time on the Google Account connections page or the Google security settings page.
  • Publication history can be deleted in the Application (one record or the whole history). Deleting the .video-publisher folder and the "video-publisher" entries in Windows Credential Manager removes all Application data from your computer.
  • Videos already published stay on the platforms; delete them there if you wish.

7. Children's Privacy

Video Publisher is not intended for children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy will be updated when the Application's features or data practices change, for example if a server component is added. The latest version is published on this page with its date.
