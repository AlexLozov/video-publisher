Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Video Publisher ("the Application") handles information when you use our desktop application.

1. Information We Process

Video Publisher may process the following information to provide its features:

  • Video files selected by the user for publishing.
  • Video titles, descriptions, tags and scheduling information.
  • Account authorization tokens required to connect social media platforms.
  • Basic account and channel information provided by connected platforms.

2. How We Use Information

Information is used to provide video scheduling, account connection and publishing functionality. The Application requests access to third-party platforms only to perform features authorized by the user.

3. Third-Party Services

Video Publisher may integrate with third-party services, including YouTube and Facebook. When you connect an account, the relevant platform processes information according to its own privacy policy.

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Meta Privacy Policy

4. Data Storage and Security

Video Publisher is a desktop application. Account credentials and application data may be stored on the user's device, depending on the application configuration. Users are responsible for protecting access to their devices.

5. Data Sharing

Video content and related metadata are sent to the social media platform selected by the user when the user requests publication.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Users can remove connected accounts and delete locally stored application data using the available application settings or by removing the relevant local files. Users can also revoke Google account access at: Google Account Connections .

7. Children's Privacy

Video Publisher is not intended for children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the Application's features or data practices change. The latest version will be published on this page.
